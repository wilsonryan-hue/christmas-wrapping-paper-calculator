How it works

For a box with length L, width W and height H, the paper goes right round the box (2 x (W + H), plus 2 cm so the edges overlap) and runs past each end by half the smaller side of the box end (L plus the smaller of W and H in total). The calculator tries all three ways of turning the box and keeps the one that uses the least paper from your roll width. A present too wide to wrap in any direction is flagged instead of counted. The spare allowance is added on top, and the rolls to buy are the total divided by the roll length, rounded up.

An estimate only: odd shapes are best entered as the smallest box that would hold them. Check the size on the roll label before you buy. To use less paper, put small presents in bags, or use brown paper or fabric. Foil and glitter paper usually cannot be recycled; check your own council's guidance.
